**A New Year’s Fantasy Inspired by the Play by N. Ptushkina**

A heartwarming, funny and deeply moving New Year’s story about how real miracles happen when you least expect them. An unexpected knock at the door on the eve of the holiday can turn an ordinary world upside down, bring new hope and change the course of someone’s life.

This staged reading is filled with gentle humour, the magic of the New Year season and genuine warmth. A charming and touching story that will make you smile, restore your faith in better things and leave no one indifferent.

**Running time:** 1 hour 25 minutes (no intermission).
